Strategic Projects

Building What Matters.

SWEP is interested in projects capable of creating meaningful economic impact: projects that strengthen energy security, expand infrastructure and support long-term growth in the economies they serve.

Perspective

Significance Demands Structure.

Projects of national and regional significance bring together capital, infrastructure, technology and public interest, often across borders. They demand patience, careful structuring and partners who understand both the commercial and the strategic dimensions of what is being built.

SWEP’s interest lies in opportunities of this kind: projects with sound fundamentals, credible sponsors and the potential to create value that endures well beyond a single transaction.

Areas of interest

Where Impact Is Built.

Seven areas define the kinds of projects SWEP considers.

  1. 01

    Energy Security

    Supply, storage and infrastructure that strengthen the reliability of energy for economies and industries.

  2. 02

    Industrial Development

    Projects that build productive capacity and support the growth of industrial sectors.

  3. 03

    Infrastructure Expansion

    Transport, logistics and energy infrastructure that enables trade and economic activity.

  4. 04

    International Trade

    Opportunities that connect producers and markets across borders.

  5. 05

    Economic Growth

    Investment that supports employment, regional development and long-term prosperity.

  6. 06

    Technology Deployment

    Practical deployment of technologies that improve efficiency, resilience and competitiveness.

  7. 07

    Long-Term Value Creation

    Projects structured to create value over decades rather than market cycles.

SWEP’s role

A Role Shaped by Each Transaction.

SWEP’s role varies by transaction. Depending on the opportunity, SWEP may participate in one or more of the following capacities.

  • Strategic Investor

    Taking a direct interest in projects aligned with SWEP’s areas of focus.

  • Capital Partner

    Working alongside sponsors and institutions on the capital requirements of a project.

  • Project-Development Partner

    Contributing to the development of a project from concept toward implementation.

  • Transaction Coordinator

    Coordinating qualified stakeholders, advisers and counterparties through a transaction.

  • Financial Structuring Participant

    Participating in the design of appropriate investment, financing or partnership structures.

Considerations

What SWEP Looks For.

Details of individual opportunities are discussed with qualified counterparties in confidence.

Sound fundamentals
A clear commercial case, realistic assumptions and an identifiable market.
Credible participants
Sponsors, counterparties and advisers with the standing to see a project through.
Appropriate structure
An investment, financing or partnership structure suited to the project and its risks.
Lasting impact
The potential to create economic value that endures beyond the transaction itself.
