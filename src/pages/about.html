The company

Where Energy Meets Capital.

SWEP connects strategic opportunities with sophisticated capital, institutional relationships and global execution capability.

Significant energy and investment opportunities rarely fail for lack of ambition. They falter for lack of structure, credible counterparties or the right capital. SWEP exists to bring those elements together.

The company operates across oil and gas, commodities, infrastructure, strategic investment and international capital, working with qualified companies, financial institutions, institutional investors and professional advisers. Its approach is deliberate: opportunities are evaluated on their fundamentals, structured appropriately and advanced only with the right partners in place.

Corporate philosophy

Discipline Before Scale.

Scale is the result of discipline, never a substitute for it. A small number of principles apply to every relationship SWEP enters and every transaction it considers.

  1. I

    Integrity

    Relationships built on professionalism and trust.

    SWEP aims to be a counterparty others can rely on: clear in its commitments and consistent in how it meets them.

  2. II

    Discipline

    Structured evaluation before capital commitment.

    Opportunities are assessed on their fundamentals, their counterparties and their requirements before capital is committed.

  3. III

    Confidentiality

    Respect for sensitive commercial and financial information.

    Information entrusted to SWEP is handled with discretion and shared only where appropriate.

  4. IV

    Compliance

    Appropriate legal, regulatory and counterparty due diligence.

    Compliance is treated as a precondition of every transaction, not a step to be completed afterwards.

  5. V

    Long-Term Thinking

    Building relationships and businesses designed to endure.

    Success is measured over years and relationships, not over individual transactions.
