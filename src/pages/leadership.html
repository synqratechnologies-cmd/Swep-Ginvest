Thomas E. Kelly

President

Exploration & Production · Energy Services · Midstream · Investment · Midland, Texas

Executive profile

More than four decades building and leading businesses in the oil and gas industry, from exploration and production to energy services and midstream.

Thomas E. Kelly graduated from Baylor University with a Bachelor of Arts degree in 1977. He began his career with First National Bank of Granbury and, in 1979, moved to Midland, Texas, to join First National Bank of Midland.

In 1981 he co-founded Baytech Inc., an oil and gas exploration and production company with operations in West Texas, New Mexico, Oklahoma, Wyoming and North Dakota. His experience spans the full life of an oil and gas asset: developing the prospect, acquiring the drilling acreage, raising funds for drilling, supervising completion, and managing revenue distribution and the operation of the producing asset.

Across his career, Mr. Kelly has developed a core group of long-term investors, relationships with financial institutions and a broad-based advisory team, partnering with operating companies that apply advanced technologies to the development of natural gas and oil reserves. His activities have focused on well-known productive areas including the Permian Basin, the Bakken, the Bend Arch of North Central Texas and South America.
