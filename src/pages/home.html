About SWEP

The Power of Energy. The Discipline of Capital.

SWEP connects strategic opportunities with sophisticated capital, institutional relationships and global execution capability.

The company operates at the intersection of energy, strategic investment, commodities, infrastructure, international capital and partnerships. Each is necessary. None is sufficient on its own.

Value is created where they are brought together with care: where a credible opportunity meets appropriate capital, qualified counterparties and the discipline required to see a transaction through to completion.

  • Energy

    Oil and gas, petroleum products, commodities and the infrastructure that moves them to market.

  • Capital

    Strategic and institutional capital, deployed through structure, diligence and disciplined evaluation.

  • Partnerships

    Long-term relationships with qualified companies, financial institutions, investors and advisers.

Institutional Capital

Connecting Opportunity With Capital.

Significant transactions require structure, disciplined due diligence, credible counterparties and financial institutions capable of supporting complex international transactions. SWEP advances qualified opportunities through five deliberate stages.

  1. 01

    Origination

    Identify strategically important opportunities.

  2. 02

    Assessment

    Evaluate commercial fundamentals, counterparties and project requirements.

  3. 03

    Structuring

    Develop an appropriate investment, financing or partnership structure.

  4. 04

    Due Diligence

    Conduct legal, financial, commercial and regulatory review.

  5. 05

    Execution

    Coordinate qualified stakeholders through documentation, financing and implementation.

Every transaction remains subject to due diligence, legal and regulatory review, contractual documentation and internal approvals.
